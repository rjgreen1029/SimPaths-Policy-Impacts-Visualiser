Funders & partners

The SimPaths Policy Impacts Visualiser was developed by researchers at the University of Glasgow, using outputs from the SimPaths microsimulation model.

PHI UK — Policy Modelling for Health

The visualiser was built as part of the Policy Modelling for Health research group, which is funded by UK Research and Innovation (UKRI).

SimPaths

SimPaths is an open-source, dynamic microsimulation model developed by the Centre for Microsimulation and Policy Analysis (CeMPA) at the University of Essex. Details of the funding that supports SimPaths are on the SimPaths website.
